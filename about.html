About Talk The Taste

Helping brands grow beyond limits, from Dubai to Irvine.

Talk The Taste is a creative and digital agency built around one idea: senior people doing the work, not junior teams and no outsourcing in between. We handle marketing strategy, social media, branding, web design, mobile apps, custom software and video production — end to end, for brands in the UAE and the United States.

Founder & Project Lead

The person who reviews every enquiry.

Umair Dada

Founder & Project Lead, Talk The Taste

Umair founded Talk The Taste on a simple premise: strategy and execution should sit with the same person, not get lost between a strategist, a designer and an account manager who never talk to each other. He holds a Marketing Management and Digital Strategy Graduate Certificate from Harvard University, along with a run of Harvard executive courses covering AI in marketing, brand strategy, consumer neuromarketing, marketing analytics and product management — the toolkit behind how TTT plans campaigns, not just how it executes them.

What sets him apart from most agency leads is the other half of his background: several years building backend systems and full-stack products, including API work on a multi-client SaaS platform and systems engineered to handle event crowds of 100,000+ in real time. He holds a Bachelor's in Information Technology from Middlesex University Dubai (First Class Honours) and is completing a Master's in Computer Science at MIT. That means the person shaping your marketing strategy can also architect and ship the website, app or platform it runs on.
