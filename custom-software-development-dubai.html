Custom vs Off-the-Shelf

When a generic tool stops being the right answer.

Off-the-shelf gets you started fast

Generic SaaS tools are cheaper to start with and require no development time. They work well until your process becomes specific enough that the tool forces constant workarounds.

Custom software pays off at that point

Once workarounds start costing more time than a proper build would, custom software built around your actual workflow becomes the better investment — and it's an asset you own outright, not a subscription you're locked into.

Architected to scale, not just to launch

We design the database and backend for the growth you expect over the following one to two years, so scaling from 100 to 10,000 users doesn't require starting over.
