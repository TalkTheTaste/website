Content Creation FAQ

Questions Dubai brands ask before hiring us.

How much does content creation cost in Dubai?

A monthly package covering a shoot plus a set content volume typically starts from AED 3,000–8,000.

What's the difference from social media management?

Content creation produces the raw material — photography, copy, design. Social media management handles posting and community management.

Do you write copy as well as produce visuals?

Yes, captions, website copy and brand storytelling are produced alongside the visual content, matched to your brand voice.

How often do you deliver content?

Most packages run on a monthly shoot producing a batch of material, scheduled across the following weeks.

Can you produce content for a specific launch?

Yes, one-off campaign content is scoped and quoted as a standalone project separate from an ongoing package.

Who owns the content after delivery?

You do — all photography, copy and design files are delivered as final assets you own outright.
