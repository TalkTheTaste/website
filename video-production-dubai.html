How We Work

A production process built to protect the shoot day.

  1. Concept & script (Week 1)

    Treatment, shot list and shooting schedule agreed before crew is booked, so the shoot day runs to plan.

  2. Pre-production (Week 1–2)

    Talent, locations, permits and equipment confirmed, with a call sheet shared ahead of the shoot.

  3. Production day

    Filming executed against the shot list, with contingency time built in for setup and location changes.

  4. Edit & delivery (1–2 weeks)

    First cut, one round of revisions, and final delivery across every aspect ratio the placement needs.

Video Production FAQ

Questions Dubai brands ask before booking a shoot.

How much does video production cost in Dubai?

A single brand film or commercial shoot typically starts from AED 8,000–15,000 depending on crew size, location and post-production complexity.

How long does a video shoot take?

A single-location shoot usually takes half a day to a full day. Multi-location commercials with talent typically run two to four days including pre-production.

Do you handle post-production and colour grading?

Yes, editing, sound design and colour grading are included on every shoot, delivered in the aspect ratios each placement needs.

Can you produce recurring content, not just one-off shoots?

Yes, many clients run a monthly shoot day that produces a batch of content we then edit and schedule across the following weeks.

Do you supply talent, locations and permits?

We can source talent and locations and advise on Dubai filming permit requirements, though permit applications are quoted separately.

What equipment do you shoot on?

Cinema-grade mirrorless and cinema cameras, gimbal and slider rigs, and a lighting kit sized to the location and shoot.
