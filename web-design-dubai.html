Technical Standards

What we commit to on every build.

Core Web Vitals targets

Every site we ship targets a Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds and a Cumulative Layout Shift under 0.1 — measured on real mobile devices, not just desktop, before we call a build complete.

Payment and reservation integrations

We integrate with payment gateways available in the UAE, along with reservation and CRM platforms already in use by Dubai restaurants, clinics and service businesses, so the website plugs into your existing operations instead of creating a second system to manage.

What's included versus chargeable

Web Design FAQ

Questions Dubai businesses ask before hiring us.

How much does a website cost in Dubai?

Brochure sites typically start from AED 6,000–12,000, booking-enabled sites from AED 12,000–25,000, and e-commerce builds from AED 20,000 upward.

How long does a website project take?

A brochure or landing-page site typically takes three to five weeks. Booking-integrated or e-commerce builds usually take six to ten weeks.

What platform do you build on?

Hand-coded static builds for speed-critical marketing sites, or Shopify and WordPress for stores and content-heavy sites needing in-house editing.

Who owns the website after launch?

You do — full code, hosting access and licensed assets transfer to you on final payment, with no retainer lock-in required to keep the site live.

Is hosting and maintenance included?

Hosting setup is included in every build. Ongoing maintenance and content updates are available as an optional monthly plan.

Is SEO included?

Technical SEO fundamentals — clean URLs, meta tags, structured data and sitemap — ship with every site. Ongoing content SEO is a separate service.
