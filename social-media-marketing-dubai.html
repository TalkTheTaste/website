How We Work

A monthly cycle, not a one-off content dump.

  1. Strategy & calendar (Week 1)

    Content pillars, campaign angles and a posting calendar built around your business cycle, not a generic template reused across clients.

  2. Shoot & produce (Week 1–2)

    Photography and short-form video production covering the month's planned content in a single organized session.

  3. Post & manage (Ongoing)

    Scheduled posting, community management and campaign monitoring run continuously through the month.

  4. Report & adjust (End of month)

    Performance review against the plan, with next month's content and spend adjusted against what actually worked.

Platform Approach

What we know about running social in the UAE.

Platform-by-platform breakdown

Instagram and TikTok carry most of the weight for consumer brands in Dubai. LinkedIn matters for B2B and professional services. Snapchat still drives meaningful reach with a younger UAE audience — a platform most Dubai agencies skip entirely, which is exactly why it's often underpriced attention.

Organic versus paid budget split

We typically recommend weighting spend toward paid once organic content is validated — testing angles organically first, then putting budget behind what's already proven to convert, rather than boosting everything equally.

Community management scope

Comments and DMs are answered within an agreed response window as part of every retainer. In a market where customers expect a WhatsApp-speed reply, a slow DM response is a direct loss of enquiries.

Social Media FAQ

Questions Dubai brands ask before hiring us.

How much does social media management cost in Dubai?

Retainers typically range from AED 3,500 to AED 12,000 per month depending on post volume and whether a content shoot is included.

How many posts do you produce per month?

Most packages run between 8 and 20 posts per month across feed, stories and reels, scoped to your platform mix.

Is ad spend included in the management fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or TikTok on top of the management fee, so you see exactly what's going to media versus service.

How often do you report on performance?

A monthly report covering reach, engagement, follower growth and campaign results, with next month's recommendations.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram and TikTok for most brands, LinkedIn for B2B, and Snapchat where it matters for reaching a younger UAE audience.

Do you handle community management and DMs?

Yes — replying to comments and DMs within an agreed response window is included in every retainer.
