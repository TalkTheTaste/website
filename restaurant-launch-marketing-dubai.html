Timing

The launch work starts before the doors open.

  1. Weeks 6-8 before opening

    Positioning, offer, audience, content needs and booking path are fixed.

  2. Weeks 3-5 before opening

    Food photography, reels, Google Business Profile and teaser content production move into execution.

  3. Opening week

    Influencer visits, paid social, social posting and review prompts are coordinated around real service capacity.

  4. First 30 days

    Campaigns shift from awareness to repeat visits, delivery orders and review volume.

Launch FAQ

Questions operators ask before opening.

When should restaurant launch marketing start?

Restaurant launch marketing should usually start six to eight weeks before opening so content, listings, influencers and booking links are ready before demand arrives.

What is included in a restaurant opening campaign?

A launch campaign usually includes strategy, food photography, teaser content, Google Business Profile setup, influencer seeding, paid campaigns and booking flow checks.

Do UAE influencers need a licence?

Yes. Paid influencer activity in the UAE requires the appropriate licence or permit, and we check this before campaign activity goes live.
