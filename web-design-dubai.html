Direct Answer

What is included in website development in Dubai?

Website development in Dubai should include responsive design, page build, technical SEO, Core Web Vitals optimization, secure hosting setup, contact forms, WhatsApp click tracking, analytics, sitemap submission and any booking, payment or CRM integrations needed by the business. The goal is not only a good-looking website; it is a fast, crawlable site that turns visitors into enquiries.

What should a Dubai business website include?

A Dubai business website should clearly show services, location, contact details, WhatsApp access, proof of work, FAQs, pricing or starting ranges where possible, and trust signals such as reviews, case studies, licences, portfolio examples or company details.

Technical Standards

What we commit to on every build.

Core Web Vitals targets

Every site we ship targets a Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds and a Cumulative Layout Shift under 0.1 — measured on real mobile devices, not just desktop, before we call a build complete.

Payment and reservation integrations

We integrate with payment gateways available in the UAE, along with reservation and CRM platforms already in use by Dubai restaurants, clinics and service businesses, so the website plugs into your existing operations instead of creating a second system to manage.

What's included versus chargeable

Design, development, one round of revisions, hosting setup and SEO fundamentals are included in every quoted price. Ongoing content updates, additional integrations added after launch and paid ad management are scoped separately.
