How We Work

A four to six week process from strategy to final files.

  1. Discovery & strategy (Week 1)

    Positioning workshop, competitor review and audience definition that every design decision gets tested against.

  2. Concept exploration (Weeks 2–3)

    Two to three distinct visual directions presented, not incremental variations of a single idea.

  3. Refinement (Week 4)

    One full round of revisions on the selected direction, tightened into a final logo suite and color system.

  4. Guidelines & handover (Weeks 5–6)

    Brand guidelines document, source files and launch templates delivered, with a walkthrough call included.

New Brand vs Rebrand

The process changes depending on what you're protecting.

New brand

A new brand starts from a blank page — there's no existing recognition to protect, so we can move faster through concept exploration and commit to a bolder direction.

Rebrand of an existing identity

A rebrand has to balance change with continuity. We audit what's already working — a color, a mark, a tone customers already associate with you — before touching the identity, and phase the rollout across touchpoints to avoid confusing existing customers.

Trademark considerations in the UAE

We flag naming or visual conflicts during the design process and can refer you to a UAE trademark agent before you commit to a final identity, though we don't file trademark applications directly.

Branding FAQ

Questions Dubai brands ask before hiring us.

What is delivered in a branding project?

A logo suite, color palette, typography system, brand guidelines document and launch-ready templates for social, print and signage.

What file formats do you provide?

Layered source files (AI, PSD or Figma), print-ready PDFs, and web-ready PNG and SVG exports of every logo variation.

How long does a branding project take?

A full identity project typically takes four to six weeks from kickoff to final files. A rebrand can take longer depending on scope.

How much does branding cost in Dubai?

A full identity project typically starts from AED 8,000, with naming and packaging scoped separately.

What's the difference between a rebrand and a new brand?

A new brand has no existing recognition to protect. A rebrand has to balance change with continuity across existing customer touchpoints.

Do you handle trademark registration?

We flag naming or visual conflicts during design and can refer you to a UAE trademark agent, though we don't file applications directly.
