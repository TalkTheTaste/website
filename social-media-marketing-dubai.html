03 / Before we begin

A few useful details.

What is included in a monthly package?

The agreed number of posts, reels, stories and shoots, plus the management and reporting scope. The pricing page sets out the available packages.

Is advertising spend included?

Ad spend and paid campaign management are agreed separately. We keep paid and organic reporting distinct.

Who approves the content?

Your nominated contact reviews the calendar and content before publication. Approval dates are built into the schedule.
