01 / Scope

What we work on.

We agree the deliverables, responsibilities and review stages before work begins.

01

Positioning & audience

Clarify your offer, the people you want to reach and the reasons they would choose you.

02

Marketing audit

Review your channels, content, website and customer journey. Identify gaps before adding more activity.

03

Campaign & budget plan

Set objectives, choose channels and agree where the time and budget should go.

04

Reporting & review

Agree which outcomes matter, how to track them and when to revisit the plan.

The proposal sets out the final scope, timeline and fee. Any production costs, subscriptions or media spend are agreed separately where relevant.

A few useful details.

Yes. Strategy can be a standalone project with an agreed handover, or the starting point for ongoing work.

What do you need from us?

No. We work across industries. Restaurant teams can also use our dedicated restaurant marketing service.
