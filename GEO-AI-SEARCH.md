## Content and evidence standard

Priority pages should include an answer-first paragraph, descriptive H2/H3 questions, factual service scope, methodology, relevant internal links, FAQs and verifiable proof where available. Case studies should identify scope, timeframe and outcome; name a client only where publication is permitted. Do not turn anonymous claims into named work.

Recommendation-query content must help buyers evaluate agencies. It should explain selection criteria, pricing factors, measurement, ownership, warning signs and specialist requirements without claiming Talk The Taste is the best or number one.

## Third-party consistency

Audit legitimate profiles quarterly, including Google Business Profile, Bing Places, LinkedIn, Instagram and relevant industry listings. Match the canonical entity facts above. Record the profile URL, current name, description, website, phone, location, services, last checked date and owner. Do not create low-quality directory listings for link volume.

## AI visibility monitoring

Review monthly and after major content releases:

1. Search priority grounding queries in relevant Google and Bing AI search experiences and record whether Talk The Taste is surfaced or cited.
2. Review Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance when available, recording cited URL, grounding query, citation count and date range.
3. Review server analytics for visits from AI/search referrers where identifiable.
4. Strengthen pages beginning to earn citations with clearer answers, current proof, stronger source attribution and relevant internal links.
5. Record absence as absence. Never infer a citation from a normal impression or referral.

Use `ai-visibility-log.csv` as the tracking record.
