Before you choose

A few useful answers.

What is included in Social Essentials?

For AED 1,999 per month, you receive 8 designed posts including up to 2 carousels, 2 edited reels up to 30 seconds each, 8 Instagram stories, monthly content planning, captions and hashtags, scheduling and publishing, basic content strategy and a performance report. Management covers 1 social media platform, with 1 revision round per deliverable.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Social Essentials has a 3-month minimum commitment. SEO plans also have a 3-month minimum commitment. Other project and management terms are agreed in your proposal.

Do I need to supply photos and video?

Yes. Social Essentials uses client-provided original photos and footage. On-site shoots are not included in this package. Shoot-inclusive social packages and separate UAE shoot sessions are available.

Are the carousels additional to the eight posts?

No. Up to two carousels are included within the eight monthly social media posts.

Are ads support and SEO included?

Social Essentials does not include ads support or basic SEO. Signature includes limited ads support and standard SEO optimization. Advertising spend is separate from management fees. Additional services can be scoped on request.

Can we work together outside the UAE?

Yes. Social Essentials uses your supplied photos and footage, so it can be delivered remotely. On-site shoot sessions listed here are available in the UAE only.

How do we get started?

Book your free consultation through the contact form. We will confirm the package, materials needed and next steps before work starts.
